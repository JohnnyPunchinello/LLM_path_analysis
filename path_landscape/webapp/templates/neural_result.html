Agent 4 Path representation

The path landscape: input → output paths, clustered by similarity

The path landscape is the distribution of routes from INPUT to OUTPUT, grouped into similarity modes. Two routes are similar if they share many of the same nodes and edges (composite edge-Jaccard + longest-common-subsequence overlap). Each mode in the figure below is one horizontal band: the bold centerline is the cluster's representative route, the faint translucent lines around it are the other members of the cluster (the bundle), and the prominent amber circles mark the shared anchor units — units that appear in every member route of the mode and are therefore its structural signature. Solid = feedforward-only; dashed = traverses at least one recurrent edge.

Show every individual route (per-route lane view)

Per-route view: one lane per distinct route, grouped by cluster with a left-margin bracket. Useful for inspecting specific routes that the bundle view groups together.
